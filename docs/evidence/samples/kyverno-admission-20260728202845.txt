=== honest caveat: PolicyReport background scan vs. admission enforcement ===
`kubectl get policyreport -A` at this same timestamp shows FAIL=1 for kube-system pods
(cilium, coredns, kube-proxy, kube-apiserver, etc.) and for pods in namespaces that carry a
working ClusterPolicy `exclude` (velero, and briefly observability before its own fix
landed). This does not indicate an enforcement gap — kube-system is excluded from the
Kyverno *admission webhook* by chart default (documented in
infrastructure/kyverno/policies/require-pod-security-restricted.yaml's scope note) but
Kyverno's separate background scanner still evaluates and reports on it for visibility,
and the scanner appears to re-scan on its own periodic interval rather than instantly on
policy change, so newly-excluded namespaces can show stale FAIL rows for a while after a
fix ships. The admission-time enforcement above — the mechanism that actually blocks or
allows a request — is what was verified directly, repeatedly, against real workloads
throughout Phase 4 (a rejected privileged test pod, and real Velero backups/PVC
provisioning succeeding through their respective exceptions). PolicyReport accuracy as a
reporting surface is a known, live gap, not silently glossed over here.

=== honest caveat: audit-mode is not fully reliable on this Cilium version ===
Three separate times in this segmentation pass, a CiliumNetworkPolicy carrying the
`policy.cilium.io/audit-mode: "true"` annotation was observed genuinely enforcing
(real DROPPED verdicts, not just logged) rather than staying non-enforcing as the
annotation is documented to do: loki's kube-apiserver egress (observability
namespace), openebs's Google-telemetry egress (shown above -- caught DURING
audit-mode testing, not after enforcing), and kube-system/coredns during its own
fix cycle. In two of the three cases this happened to match the intended final
policy anyway; in coredns's case it broke cluster DNS for several minutes during
testing before being caught and fixed. Every namespace in this pass was still
verified with a full audit-mode window AND a post-enforcement functional test, but
the annotation itself should be treated as advisory on this Cilium version, not a
hard guarantee of non-enforcement.

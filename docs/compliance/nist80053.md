# NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5: control mapping

**NIST Special Publication 800-53 Revision 5**, *Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations*.
Control text below is quoted from NIST's official machine-readable catalog
([usnistgov/oscal-content](https://github.com/usnistgov/oscal-content/tree/main/nist.gov/SP800-53/rev5)), a US Government work. Quotations are
**shortened** and organization-defined parameters are shown as `[organization-defined parameter]`; read the full control at
[csrc.nist.gov](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/cprt/catalog#/cprt/framework/version/SP_800_53_5_1_1/home).

This is a **representative subset** (not all ~1,000 controls) and the PE (physical) family is excluded because a cloud provider is assumed
([crosswalk scope](../06-nist-800-53-crosswalk.md)). The hand-maintained crosswalk remains the source of record; this page adds the control wording.

## AC: Access control

### AC-2: Account Management {#ac-2}

> Define and document the types of accounts allowed and specifically prohibited for use within the system; Assign account managers; Require [organization-defined parameter] for group and role membership; Specify: Authorized users of the system; Group and role membership ...

- **Implemented by:** Keycloak federated to OpenLDAP; groups claim
- **Evidence:** [keycloak-ldap-oidc-verification](https://github.com/Utility-SOC/dora-blueprint/blob/main/docs/evidence/samples/keycloak-ldap-oidc-verification-20260729043900.txt)
- **Status:** Partial
- **Caveat:** No account lifecycle or review process; two toy users

### AC-3: Access Enforcement {#ac-3}

> Enforce approved authorizations for logical access to information and system resources in accordance with applicable access control policies.

- **Implemented by:** Argo CD OIDC with RBAC `policy.csv`
- **Evidence:** [argocd-rbac-verification](https://github.com/Utility-SOC/dora-blueprint/blob/main/docs/evidence/samples/argocd-rbac-verification-20260729045300.txt)
- **Status:** Implemented
- **Caveat:** Verified through the API, not a browser login

## AU: Audit and accountability

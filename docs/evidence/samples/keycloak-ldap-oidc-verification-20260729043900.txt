This is the concrete evidence the eventual RBAC decisions in Argo CD/Grafana depend on: the
"groups" claim is what argocd-rbac-cm's `g, platform-admins, role:admin` policy line and
Grafana's role_attribute_path both match against. Without this claim actually present in a
real issued token, an RBAC policy referencing group names would be syntactically valid but
functionally inert -- checked here before wiring either downstream consumer, not after.

Note: `directAccessGrantsEnabled` was temporarily flipped to `true` on the argocd client
specifically to run this password-grant verification request, then flipped back to `false`
immediately after (`infrastructure/keycloak/configure-realm.sh` creates both clients with
only the standard authorization-code flow enabled, matching how a real browser login
actually works) -- this was a one-off verification tool, not the client's real posture.

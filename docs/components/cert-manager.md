## What it is, in plain English

A **TLS certificate** proves a server's identity and enables encrypted connections (the padlock in a browser). A **certificate authority (CA)** signs certificates; everyone who trusts the CA trusts what it signs. Best practice uses two levels: an **offline Root CA** that is kept away from any network and used rarely, and an **Intermediate CA** that does the day-to-day signing. **cert-manager** automates requesting, issuing and renewing certificates inside Kubernetes.

## Why it is here

Encryption in transit is required by DORA Art. 9(2) and NIS2 Art. 21(2)(h). A flat self-signed issuer would work but would not show the real discipline of a protected Root key.

## How this repository uses it

- Root CA (RSA 4096, 10 years) and Intermediate CA (RSA 4096, 5 years) generated by following [the runbook](../runbooks/pki-root-ca-issuance.md) in a `--network none` container.
- Only the Intermediate key goes into the cluster, as a SOPS-encrypted secret; a `ca`-type `ClusterIssuer` uses it.
- Real leaf certificates for Argo CD and Grafana; the platform CA is distributed to drills so they verify TLS properly.
- Sync waves: cert-manager (0), issuer (1), certificates (2).

## If it fails

Existing certificates keep working until they expire; new ones cannot be issued or renewed. **Expiry monitoring is not built yet (Phase 18).**

## What we learned the hard way

Real problems found by running this component, not by reading about it (from the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Utility-SOC/dora-blueprint/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)):

- The chart defaults leader-election to `kube-system`, so `global.leaderElection.namespace` had to be set.
- Grafana's HTTPS mode broke its default health probes, which do not parameterise the scheme.
- The Root CA is due for rotation in about 5 years; the runbook records the plan.

## Limits and honest caveats

- After the key loss the CA was **re-issued from scratch**; every certificate chained to the old one is invalid. This is recorded in the [secrets re-key PR](https://github.com/Utility-SOC/dora-blueprint/pull/1).
- A Root key in a stopped container is not an HSM or a witnessed ceremony.
